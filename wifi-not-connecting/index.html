Phone will not connect to Wi-Fi

Check with a second device first. If nothing can use the Wi-Fi, the router is the problem; if only your phone fails, the phone is.

What you seeAuthentication error occurred / Saved, secured / Couldn't get IP address / Connected, no internet / Incorrect password

1. Which side is at fault?

Try another phone or a laptop on the same Wi-Fi.

  • Nothing connects, or nothing has internet: restart the router. Unplug it, wait a minute, plug in, wait for the lights. If the internet light is red or off, the line is down: call the provider.
  • Other devices are fine: the trouble is in your phone. Continue.

2. Quick steps on the phone

  1. Wi-Fi off and on. Flight mode on and off.
  2. Restart the phone.
  3. Move closer to the router for the test.

3. Forget the network and join again

This fixes most password and "Saved" loops.

  • Android: Settings › Network & internet › Internet › the gear beside the network › Forget. Samsung: Settings › Connections › Wi-Fi › gear › Forget.
  • iPhone: Settings › Wi-Fi › the (i) beside the network › Forget This Network.

Then choose the network and type the password again, carefully: it is case-sensitive, and 0/O and 1/l are easily confused. Tap the eye icon to see what you typed.

4. Match the message to the cause

MessageLikely causeFix
Authentication error / Incorrect passwordWrong password, or the router's security type does not suit the phoneRetype the password. In the router, use WPA2 or WPA2/WPA3 mixed
Saved, but will not connectThe router refused or ignored the phoneForget and rejoin; restart the router; check the router's device limit and MAC filter
Obtaining IP address... / Couldn't get IP addressThe router did not hand out an addressRestart the router; forget and rejoin
Connected, no internetThe link to the router is fine; the internet beyond it is notRestart the router; check the line; turn off VPN and Private DNS on the phone
Sign in to networkA hotel, cafe or office login page is waitingOpen the browser and visit any plain website to bring up the sign-in page

5. Phone settings that interfere

  • Random MAC address. Routers with a MAC filter, and college or office networks that register devices, may refuse the made-up address a phone shows by default. In the network's settings choose Use device MAC (Android: Privacy) or turn off Private Wi-Fi Address (iPhone), for that network only.
  • VPN and Private DNS off to test.
  • Battery saver can drop Wi-Fi when the screen is off.
  • Date and time set automatically.
  • Android "Switch to mobile data automatically" (or Wi-Fi Assist on iPhone: Settings › Mobile Service, at the bottom) moves the phone off weak Wi-Fi. Turn it off if the phone keeps leaving your Wi-Fi.

6. Reset network settings

Android: Settings › System › Reset options › Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth. iPhone: Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › Reset › Reset Network Settings. All saved Wi-Fi passwords are forgotten, so have yours ready.

7. Router settings worth checking

Log in to the router from a device that still connects (the address and login are on its sticker).

  • Band. 5 GHz is fast but short-ranged; 2.4 GHz reaches further. An older phone may see only 2.4 GHz. Give the two bands different names so that you can choose.
  • Security. WPA2-PSK (AES), or WPA2/WPA3 mixed. Avoid WEP and "WPA3 only" with older phones.
  • Channel. Auto.
  • Device limit and MAC filter. Some routers cap the number of devices, or allow only listed ones.
  • Firmware. Install the update if the router offers one.

8. Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting

  • Weak signal: move the router to a central, open, high spot, or add a mesh unit or repeater.
  • Too many neighbours on 2.4 GHz: use 5 GHz near the router.
  • Forget other saved networks nearby that the phone keeps hopping to.
  • Update the phone's system.
  • If it drops only on your home Wi-Fi and nowhere else, restart or replace the ageing router.

9. The Wi-Fi switch will not turn on

A Wi-Fi toggle that is greyed out or switches itself off, with Bluetooth also failing, points to a hardware fault in the phone's wireless chip, sometimes after overheating or water. Restart, update, reset network settings; if it persists, service centre.

Why does my phone say "Connected, no internet" when the laptop works?

The phone's VPN, Private DNS, a wrong date, or a stuck address is the cause. Forget the network, rejoin, and switch those settings off.

Should I use 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz?

5 GHz when you are in the same or next room as the router; 2.4 GHz further away.

For Android phones and iPhones. Written in November 2026 for Android 13 to 15 and iOS 17 and 18. Phone makers rename menus; use the search box in Settings if a name differs. Back up before any reset.
