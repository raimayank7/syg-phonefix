About Phone Fix

Phone Fix is a free set of step-by-step guides to common problems with Android phones and iPhones. Each page starts from what you notice, explains the likely causes in plain words, and gives the fixes in the order to try them, easiest and safest first.

Which phones

The Android steps use the menu names of Android 13, 14 and 15 as found on Google Pixel and phones close to plain Android, and name Samsung, Xiaomi and other makers where their menus differ. The iPhone steps are for iOS 17 and iOS 18. Phone makers rename and move settings, so a name may not match your screen exactly; the search box at the top of Settings finds any setting.

Where the content comes from

  • Every guide is our own writing. Nothing is copied from other sites or manuals.
  • The steps use only what is built into the phone: Settings, the app stores, the makers' own recovery and backup tools, and the official websites for finding or erasing a phone (android.com/find and icloud.com/find).
  • Button combinations and menu paths were written from working knowledge in November 2026. They were not each tried on every make and model for this site, and they change with system updates.
  • The site quotes no prices, warranty terms or operator charges, since these differ by maker, model and plan. Where it matters, the guide tells you to check your own warranty or operator.

Safety first

Harmless checks come before anything that changes the phone. Pages say plainly when a step erases data, and send you to the backup guide first. We do not recommend "cleaner" or "booster" apps, unofficial app files, unlocking services or opening the phone yourself. A swollen battery, a burning smell or liquid inside the phone means stop and go to an authorised service centre.

What this site is not

It is an independent help site. It is not connected with Google, Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi or any other phone maker, app maker or mobile operator; their names and product names belong to them and appear only to say what a guide is about. We cannot see your phone, so the guides are general. We offer no remote support and no repair service, and nobody is authorised to offer them in our name.
