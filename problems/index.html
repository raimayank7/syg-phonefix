Software or hardware?

A quick way to tell: does the fault survive a restart, an update and (on Android) Safe Mode? Did it begin after a drop or after getting wet? Is it limited to one physical part, such as one strip of the screen or one speaker? Yes to these points to hardware, and no setting will cure it. A fault that began after an update or a new app, and comes and goes, is software.

The steps that erase data

Only three things on this site erase your phone: a factory reset, a restore from a computer, and erase from Find My Device or Find My. Each is the last step on its page, each is marked, and each needs the Google or Apple account afterwards. Everything else is safe to try.

Which fix should I try first if I cannot find my problem?

Restart, free some storage, update the system and apps, and on Android try Safe Mode. Those four cover most software faults.

Is it worth repairing an old phone?

A new battery or charging port is inexpensive and can give an old phone years more. A screen on an old model may cost more than the phone is worth. Ask for a quotation before agreeing to any repair.
